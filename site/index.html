In daily use at Cannwood and with Heal Rewilding in Somerset.

Already working on two Somerset rewilding sites.

Canopy runs the day-to-day monitoring at Cannwood, a 400-acre rewilding estate in Somerset, and supports Heal Rewilding's survey work. Ecologists and volunteers record butterflies, birds, dragonflies and more through structured seasonal surveys, on their phones, in the field.

Satellite-tagged turtle doves — one of Britain's fastest-declining birds — have been followed from Cannwood on migration, plotted on the same maps as every other record from the estate.

A look inside

Three of the things you'd see on a call — all running today with real field data, not mock-ups.

Camera traps that know what they saw

Camera-trap images are identified automatically by AI, then confirmed with a click. Every detection lands in the same record system as your surveys — no folders of unsorted photos.

Surveys that show change over time

Structured butterfly, bird and dragonfly surveys with seasonal charts — comparable week by week and year on year, so the story of your site builds instead of sitting in spreadsheets.

GPS tracking on a live map

Satellite tags plotted over your estate map — Cannwood's turtle doves have been followed from Somerset onwards, fix by fix, alongside GPS trackers on the ground.

Built for the field, not just the office. Canopy works on a phone in a hedgerow — it tolerates patchy signal, keeps drafts safe offline, and takes an OS grid reference as easily as a pin on the map.
